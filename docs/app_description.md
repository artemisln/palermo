## Description - English

```
Night falls on Palermo. Close your eyes.

One Night in Palermo is the ultimate social deduction game — built for friend groups, game nights, and parties you won't forget.

HOW TO PLAY
Each player secretly receives their role through the device. The killers hide among the citizens, eliminating one player each night. Citizens must discuss, suspect, and vote — who do you trust?

FEATURES

• Cinematic narration — The app guides every phase of the game with atmospheric storytelling
• 6 unique roles — Hidden Killer, Revealed Killer, Detective, Doctor, Informant, and Citizen
• Lovers mechanic — Two random players are linked. If one dies, the other follows
• Bilingual — Full Greek and English support
• 5-20 players — Perfect for small groups to large parties
• Single device — No internet or multiple phones needed. One phone is all it takes
• Auto-save — Remembers your players and settings
• Customizable — Enable or disable roles based on your group size

ROLES

Hidden Killer — Nobody knows who they are. Kills every night
Revealed Killer — The Detective knows them, but nobody else does
Detective — Knows the Revealed Killer. Must convince the others
Doctor — Saves one player each night from death
Informant — Receives limited intelligence each night
Citizen — Uses instinct and discussion to find the killers

PERFECT FOR
• Game nights with friends
• Parties and gatherings
• School trips and team events
• Family game nights
• Anyone who loves strategy and psychology games

One device. One night. Who will survive?
```
